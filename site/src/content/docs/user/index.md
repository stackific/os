Stackific OS has no release yet. This guide fills in with the first one, and will cover:

- downloading an ISO and checking it against `SHA256SUMS`;
- writing it to a USB stick and booting it, with Secure Boot off;
- the live desktop: MangoWM with Noctalia, and KDE Plasma on Arch or GNOME on Fedora;
- installing alongside another OS or on a whole drive;
- snapshots and restore with snapper and btrfs-assistant.

Until then, the **Prompt Trail** shows each step of the build as it lands.
